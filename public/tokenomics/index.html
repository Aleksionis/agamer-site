AGAMER had no pre-set allocation split and no vesting schedule. According to the founder, tokens were handed out over time as rewards to active community members (giveaways, airdrops to holders, contests), and part of the supply was added as DEX liquidity. You can check the current distribution on-chain on Tonviewer.

Utility

AGAMER is the main currency of Agamer Universe, which stays on TON — earned through gameplay and utility, not a free giveaway token (channel post, 30 Jan 2026). Holdings are also used as entry requirements and tickets for community giveaways. Coin freezing is planned. Further utility details will be announced with the Agamer Universe games.

AGAMER on Solana

AGAMER on Solana launches in Q2 2027, together with the game prototype. The exact listing date is announced once the prototype is ready, together with game trailers. Supply is 999,000, the same as on TON. There are no balance snapshots. Before the Solana listing, a swap window is open: holders burn TON AGAMER at 10 to 1 (100 AGAMER on TON gives 10 on Solana), with a minimum of 100 AGAMER per swap to keep bots out. Holders of AGAMER on Solana with a minimum balance get access to the closed beta test of the game. The minimum is announced together with the listing date. Of the Solana supply, 10% is reserved for the swap, 20% goes to the liquidity pool, and 70% is locked by smart contract with public terms. The unswapped part of the swap reserve is frozen with the 70%.

There are no hubs on Solana. The coin launches there, and a big Agamer game is announced, which the team builds itself (Q2 2027 at the earliest). It is a 3D open-world game with Solana and Agamer NFTs. The first version is a prototype (playable core: character, controls, a small map, wallet connection, and NFTs shown in the game), then one polished location, then growth into a large open world with multiplayer. Agamer Universe stays on TON: mini games, giveaways and similar as before, coin freezing as planned, and a hub for games from other developers.

The TON-to-Solana swap for Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs opens after AGAMER launches on Solana, in a window announced after the Solana launch. The window closes before the prototype of the game is released. After that, swapping a TON Agamer NFT for a Solana one is no longer possible. Only NFTs of rarity Rare, Epic and Legendary can be swapped. VIP, Common and Uncommon NFTs stay on TON forever. Rare to Legendary NFTs were originally designed for the game. The swap is 1:1: the holder gives the TON NFT and receives the same one on Solana. It is one-way and cannot be reversed. Total supply stays 1,000: a part moves to Solana permanently, and a part stays on TON permanently. All Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs owned by the project and not bought are taken off sale. They will move to Solana and be available for sale only after the prototype is released.

Everyone chooses what to do with their coin: stay on TON, move to Solana, or have both. Only Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs can move to Solana, and only before the prototype of the game is released. TON AGAMER stays on TON forever as the original token. Trading and pools stay. LP tokens are burned so liquidity cannot be withdrawn.
