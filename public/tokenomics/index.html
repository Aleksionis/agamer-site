AGAMER had no pre-set allocation split and no vesting schedule. According to the founder, tokens were handed out over time as rewards to active community members (giveaways, airdrops to holders, contests), and part of the supply was added as DEX liquidity. You can check the current distribution on-chain in the top-holders table and on tonviewer.

Utility

AGAMER is the main currency of Agamer Universe, which stays on TON — earned through gameplay and utility, not a free giveaway token (channel post, 30 Jan 2026). Holdings are also used as entry requirements and tickets for community giveaways. Coin freezing is planned. Further utility details will be announced with the Agamer Universe games.

AGAMER on Solana

AGAMER on Solana launches on 19 Jan 2027. Supply is 999,000, the same as on TON. A public snapshot of TON AGAMER balances is taken on 1 Jan 2027. The burn window is 1-18 Jan 2027: holders burn TON AGAMER at 10 to 1 (100 AGAMER on TON gives 10 on Solana). Of the Solana supply, 10% is reserved for the swap, 20% goes to the liquidity pool, and 70% is locked by smart contract with public terms. The unswapped part of the swap reserve is frozen with the 70%.

There are no hubs on Solana. The coin launches there, and a big Agamer game is announced, which the team builds itself (Q2 2027 at the earliest). Agamer Universe stays on TON: mini games, giveaways and similar as before, coin freezing as planned, and a hub for games from other developers.

The TON-to-Solana Agamer NFT swap becomes possible after the AGAMER coin launches on Solana. It is announced separately and lasts until the launch of the big Agamer game. After the game launches, swapping a TON Agamer NFT for a Solana one is no longer possible. Only NFTs of rarity Rare, Epic and Legendary can be swapped. VIP, Common and Uncommon NFTs stay on TON forever. Rare to Legendary NFTs were originally designed for the game. The swap is 1:1: the holder gives the TON NFT and receives the same one on Solana. It is one-way and cannot be reversed. Total supply stays 1,000: a part moves to Solana permanently, and a part stays on TON permanently.

Everyone chooses what to do with their coin: stay on TON, move to Solana, or have both. Only Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs can move to Solana, and only until the big game launches. TON AGAMER stays on TON forever as the original token. Trading and pools stay. LP tokens are burned so liquidity cannot be withdrawn.
