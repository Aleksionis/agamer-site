AGAMER Command — In development

We've started work on our next game on Roblox. It's a team format: two teams of 4 are formed for each short battle. You bring a commander, a vehicle and a support item from your Agamer NFTs. Every Rare, Epic and Legendary line is in the game, with its own advantages. The map has land, sea and air, and space opens mid-match.

The look will follow the style of our NFTs, and it will be comfortable to play on a phone — smooth even on a mid-range device.

Each NFT has its own characteristics. A rarer commander or vehicle gives stronger bonuses, so it makes sense to rotate your collection: sell some NFTs and buy others that fit your playstyle. That also boosts trading on the secondary market.

Public servers will stay open to everyone: the same classes will also be available for Robux. Tournaments will be on private servers for Agamer NFT holders, and rewards will be issued in Agamer Universe. VIP, Common and Uncommon are not part of the combat set.

Concepts

1

AGAMER Drop Race 1.0 — Play

12–24 players on a bright city map. For 2–3 minutes AGAMER checks and gifts of different rarity fall from the sky — run, jump, stun rivals (safe PvP, no killing). Top 3 on the podium with confetti.

Why kids love it: instant action and “I got it first!”.

AGAMER: drops look like Universe checks & gifts; a rare golden check is the seasonal event. Shop: bags, auras, trails, victory emotes.

2

AGAMER Tycoon Clash

8–16 players each build a points farm — conveyors, collector bots, upgrades. Then a 2-minute raid: steal points or break generators on other bases. Most points at the whistle wins.

Why kids love it: tycoons are a top Roblox genre, plus a short base battle.

AGAMER: AGAMER Points currency, bot-farm and gift-tower buildings, hero skins, bot pets.

3

AGAMER Arena Party

A party game: 60–90-second rounds — king of the hill on the logo, capture the check, crumbling tiles, last one standing. 6–8 rounds to a final; solo or teams.

Why kids love it: quick, funny, easy to jump in for five minutes with friends.

AGAMER: branded arenas, a Unix-style commentator bot, seasonal battle pass with hero hats.

4

AGAMER Hunt Squad

Teams of 3 search a big map for hidden season tokens and can snatch them from rivals (with a cooldown, safe zones). 5–7-minute matches; the rarest haul wins.

Why kids love it: co-op with friends plus hide-and-seek style hunting.

AGAMER: tokens are a seasonal collection (just rarities, no blockchain in Roblox), lobby trading, cosmetic backpacks and titles.
