AGAMER on Roblox

Concepts · WIP
Four competitive game ideas by Unix for a younger audience. Short matches, playable on phone and PC. Money only for cosmetics, battle pass and one-match boosts — no pay-to-win.

1

AGAMER Drop Race

12–24 players on a bright city map. For 2–3 minutes AGAMER checks and gifts of different rarity fall from the sky — run, jump, push rivals (safe PvP, no killing). Top 3 on the podium with confetti.

Why kids love it: instant action and “I got it first!”.

AGAMER: drops look like Universe checks & gifts; a rare golden check is the seasonal event. Shop: bags, auras, trails, victory emotes.

2

AGAMER Tycoon Clash

8–16 players each build a points farm — conveyors, collector bots, upgrades. Then a 2-minute raid: steal points or break generators on other bases. Most points at the whistle wins.

Why kids love it: tycoons are a top Roblox genre, plus a short base battle.

AGAMER: AGAMER Points currency, bot-farm and gift-tower buildings, hero skins, bot pets.

3

AGAMER Arena Party

A party game: 60–90-second rounds — king of the hill on the logo, capture the check, crumbling tiles, last one standing. 6–8 rounds to a final; solo or teams.

Why kids love it: quick, funny, easy to jump in for five minutes with friends.

AGAMER: branded arenas, a Unix-style commentator bot, seasonal battle pass with hero hats.

4

AGAMER Hunt Squad

Teams of 3 search a big map for hidden season tokens and can snatch them from rivals (with a cooldown, safe zones). 5–7-minute matches; the rarest haul wins.

Why kids love it: co-op with friends plus hide-and-seek style hunting.

AGAMER: tokens are a seasonal collection (just rarities, no blockchain in Roblox), lobby trading, cosmetic backpacks and titles.
