▶ AGAMER Drop Race — v1.4 — Winter Update

Public version

Anyone can play for free on Roblox. Coins rain down on a giant Roman Colosseum — race to grab as many as you can before the timer runs out.

  • 62 heroes, each with a passive and an active skill. Full Legendary, Epic and Rare rebalance. Different round modes drop at random each round. Abilities work on public servers and in tournaments
  • Winter Mode ❄: snow-covered Colosseum, snowfall, Santa's sleighs pulled by reindeer instead of chariots, and slippery ice patches. Winter heroes Nivaala, Pingwix and Arctix get the Frostborn ❄ passive
  • Flood: when the water rises, 6–7 hunting Megalodons chase anyone in the water (5s respawn, no immunity)
  • Darkness: hunting Shadow Panthers stay hidden outside your vision radius (5s respawn, no immunity). Night heroes get a single Night Vision (Q) ability — ×2 / ×3 / ×4 vision for coins and panthers
  • Rush: higher speed cap (×1.75)
  • Weekly Buff: 6 heroes get −30% active cooldown. Buffs run Monday–Sunday (Bratislava). A new six is announced in @agamer_channel each Monday. The current six, all abilities and modes are in the abilities sheet
  • 4 coin tiers: Bronze x1, Silver x2, Gold x5, Platinum x10
  • Live score on screen while you run
  • Shove knocks rivals back and slows them
  • Chariots race around the arena and knock players aside — in Winter Mode they become Santa's sleighs pulled by reindeer
  • Top-3 leaderboard after every round
  • Emperor's box above the arena, with giant platinum AGAMER and Gram coins over the stands
  • Reworked phone HUD: the timer, score and coins no longer overlap, and the buttons are easier to tap
  • Smoother play on phones, spinning coins, music and a better hero select
  • Works on phone and PC

AGAMER Command — In development

We've started work on our next game on Roblox. It's a team format: two teams of 4 are formed for each short battle. You bring a commander, a vehicle and a support item from your Agamer NFTs. Every Rare, Epic and Legendary line is in the game, with its own advantages. The map has land, sea and air, and space opens mid-match.

The look will follow the style of our NFTs, and it will be comfortable to play on a phone — smooth even on a mid-range device.

Each NFT has its own characteristics. A rarer commander or vehicle gives stronger bonuses, so it makes sense to rotate your collection: sell some NFTs and buy others that fit your playstyle. That also boosts trading on the secondary market.

Public servers will stay open to everyone: the same classes will also be available for Robux. Tournaments will be on private servers for Agamer NFT holders, and rewards will be issued in Agamer Universe. VIP, Common and Uncommon are not part of the combat set.

Concepts

1

AGAMER Drop Race v1.4 — Play

Race for coins in a giant Roman Colosseum. 62 heroes with a passive and an active skill, Winter Mode with Santa's sleighs, hunting Megalodons and Shadow Panthers, four coin tiers, live score and a top-3 leaderboard after every round. Play on phone or PC.

Why kids love it: instant action and “I got it first!”.

AGAMER: public servers are free for everyone; train any time on the private server; daily tournaments for NFT hero holders are announced in our Telegram channel. The whole TOP-10 keeps all the coins they collected. On equal score, whoever reached it first ranks higher.

2

AGAMER Tycoon Clash

8–16 players each build a points farm — conveyors, collector bots, upgrades. Then a 2-minute raid: steal points or break generators on other bases. Most points at the whistle wins.

Why kids love it: tycoons are a top Roblox genre, plus a short base battle.

AGAMER: AGAMER Points currency, bot-farm and gift-tower buildings, hero skins, bot pets.

3

AGAMER Arena Party

A party game: 60–90-second rounds — king of the hill on the logo, capture the check, crumbling tiles, last one standing. 6–8 rounds to a final; solo or teams.

Why kids love it: quick, funny, easy to jump in for five minutes with friends.

AGAMER: branded arenas, a Unix-style commentator bot, seasonal battle pass with hero hats.

4

AGAMER Hunt Squad

Teams of 3 search a big map for hidden season tokens and can snatch them from rivals (with a cooldown, safe zones). 5–7-minute matches; the rarest haul wins.

Why kids love it: co-op with friends plus hide-and-seek style hunting.

AGAMER: tokens are a seasonal collection (just rarities, no blockchain in Roblox), lobby trading, cosmetic backpacks and titles.
