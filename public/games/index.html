AGAMER Drop Race — Version 1.3

Race for coins in a giant Roman Colosseum. Every hero has a passive tied to its strongest round mode and an active skill. Play the public version for free, or train on the private server at any time.

Weekly Buff: 6 heroes get −30% active cooldown, Monday–Sunday (Bratislava). A new six is announced in @agamer_channel each Monday. All abilities, modes and weekly buffs are in the abilities sheet.

Tournaments run every day on the private server and start automatically. Entry: a Rare, Epic or Legendary hero NFT, or an active hero rental in @AgamerUniverseBot → Hero Rental. The day’s schedule is posted every morning in @agamer_channel. The whole TOP-10 keeps all the coins they collect; on equal score, whoever reached it first ranks higher. Scores add up to Top Players of the Day (TOP-1 gets 3 $GRAM) and Top Players of the Week, counted Monday–Sunday (TOP-1 gets 11 $GRAM). Prizes are paid in Agamer Universe.

AGAMER Poker — Live

AGAMER Poker is live in @AgamerUniverseBot. Open the app, go to the Games section and pick a table. Tables are in AGAMER and $GRAM. Poker is a Telegram game inside Agamer Universe, not a Roblox game.

A table fee is added on top of the buy-in. The fee depends on your Agamer NFT:

Agamer NFTTable fee
No NFT10%
Common9%
Uncommon7.5%
Rare6%
Epic4%
Legendary2.5%
VIP1%

Example: a 100 AGAMER buy-in table costs 109 with Common, 106 with Rare, and 101 with VIP.

All fees go to the project wallet for running and maintaining AGAMER.
