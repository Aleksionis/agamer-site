1. Abstract

AGAMER is a community token on The Open Network (TON), launched on 20 April 2025 as the official coin of the Agamer channel. It is designed to be the main currency of Agamer Universe, a Telegram gaming hub with several games, together with the 1,000-piece Agamer NFT collection. The contract's admin rights are revoked, so supply can never be increased. AGAMER trades in 5 liquidity pools on STON.fi and DeDust.

2. Vision and problem

Many community tokens are given away through airdrops and have no lasting use. Agamer's stated approach (channel post, 30 Jan 2026) is "No meaningless airdrops": AGAMER is earned through gameplay and utility inside Agamer Universe, and Agamer NFTs give real in-game utility, where rarity matters and holders can win cash prizes, valuable items and other real-world rewards.

Long-term vision: Agamer wants to grow into a full gaming hub, Agamer Universe, where AGAMER is the main in-game currency and Agamer NFTs give status, access and real rewards. The project plans to expand into minigames and interactive challenges, to build its own art, lore and storytelling, to work with creators, artists and gaming communities, and to form cross-project partnerships in Web3 and gaming. Target audience: Telegram and TON gamers, NFT collectors and the Agamer community.

AGAMER had no pre-set allocation split and no vesting schedule. According to the founder, tokens were handed out over time as rewards to active community members (giveaways, airdrops to holders, contests), and part of the supply was added as DEX liquidity. You can check the current distribution on-chain in the top-holders table and on tonviewer.

Utility: AGAMER is the main currency of Agamer Universe. It is earned through gameplay and utility rather than meaningless airdrops, and it is meant for in-game payments and rewards. Holdings are already used as entry requirements and tickets for community giveaways (for example "100 Agamer = 1 ticket" and "1000+ Agamer to join"), and AGAMER is used for Agamer NFT upgrades and promotions through the Agamer wheel. 5. Ecosystem

  • Agamer Universe (@agameruniversebot) — the gaming hub in a Telegram bot, with multiple games inside. It runs the Agamer wheel and Agamer giveaways. Games and reward mechanics: to be announced.
  • Agamer NFT (getgems.io/agamer) — launched 4 Jul 2025, with a hard cap of 1,000 NFTs. Rarities are VIP (only 55), Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary. Special editions (Human, Universe, Fire, Unix…) are minted in small fixed batches. NFTs were airdropped to AGAMER holders, and common NFTs can be upgraded through the Agamer wheel.
  • Agamer Marketplace — launched in 2025.
  • Community — channel @agamer_channel (7,563 subscribers), chat @agamer_chat, plus the NFT and holders communities (@agamer_nft, @agamer_community).
  • Website — agamer.space, first launched in Nov 2025 and rebuilt in Oct 2026.
  • Price widgets — Android (APK) and iOS (Scriptable) home-screen widgets released in Oct 2026. They show live price, chart, pools and trades from all 5 pools.
  • @AgamerPriceBot — a Telegram bot released in Oct 2026. Its /price command sends a candlestick chart, /pools lists the pools and /trades shows recent trades (EN/RU/FA). It also posts buy alerts in @agamer_chat.
  • Sticker pack — AgamerUniverse.
