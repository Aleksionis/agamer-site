About

Agamer Universe is a Telegram ecosystem on TON built around play and collecting. Inside @agameruniversebot you'll find games (AGAMER Poker in the Games section), a built-in marketplace and auctions for Agamer NFTs and Telegram Gifts, Hero Rental for AGAMER Drop Race on Roblox, giveaways and AGAMER holds (1, 3, 6 or 12 months) — all connected by the AGAMER coin. The Agamer NFT collection is also available on Getgems.

Play AGAMER Drop Race

Our first game — version 1.3 is live on Roblox. Anyone can play the public version for free. Every hero has a passive tied to its strongest round mode and an active skill — see the Drop Race page.

Weekly Buff: 6 heroes get −30% active cooldown. Buffs run Monday–Sunday (Bratislava). A new six is announced in @agamer_channel each Monday. See all hero abilities, modes and weekly buffs in the abilities sheet.

Tournaments run every day on the private server and start automatically. Entry: a Rare, Epic or Legendary hero NFT, or an active hero rental in @AgamerUniverseBot → Hero Rental. The day’s schedule is posted every morning in @agamer_channel. The whole TOP-10 keeps all the coins they collect; on equal score, whoever reached it first ranks higher. Scores add up to Top Players of the Day (TOP-1 gets 3 $GRAM) and Top Players of the Week, counted Monday–Sunday (TOP-1 gets 11 $GRAM). Prizes are paid in Agamer Universe. You can also train on the private server at any time. Players chat in @agamer_droprace.

Our next Roblox game, AGAMER Command, is in development.

Play AGAMER Poker

AGAMER Poker is live in @AgamerUniverseBot. Open the app, go to the Games section and pick a table. Tables are in AGAMER and $GRAM. Poker is a Telegram game inside Agamer Universe and is not connected to Roblox.

A table fee is added on top of the buy-in. The fee depends on your Agamer NFT:

Agamer NFTTable fee
No NFT10%
Common9%
Uncommon7.5%
Rare6%
Epic4%
Legendary2.5%
VIP1%

Example: a 100 AGAMER buy-in table costs 109 with Common, 106 with Rare, and 101 with VIP.

All fees go to the project wallet for running and maintaining AGAMER.

Our own AGAMER blockchain is planned by the end of 2027, a fresh start with its own validators. There is no swap, bridge or migration of the current TON coin to the new chain. Any “Agamer chain swap” or “bridge” you see before an official announcement in @agamer_channel is a scam. Next year (2027) the Agamer game, the AGAMER blockchain and the AGAMER coin open to the whole world at the same moment. The game is tested inside our community first, and only the launch date will be announced. Until launch there are no further announcements about the AGAMER blockchain or the AGAMER coin.

1-year hold bonus: everyone with AGAMER in a 1-year hold in Agamer Universe receives exactly the same amount of AGAMER on the new AGAMER blockchain. This covers holds already locked and holds locked before the end of 2026. A snapshot of all 1-year holds is taken on 31.12.2026 at 23:59 (Bratislava time); each hold is linked to your Telegram account in Agamer Universe and to the wallet that made it, and either one proves it.

AGAMER Price Widget v3.2

Home-screen widgets for Android and iOS with live price in $ and Gram, chart, pools and trades. New in v3.2: fixed the home-screen trade list that could freeze after an update on some Android launchers (Xiaomi, Android 12+), so the list now updates together with the widget. The app and the widget no longer call the data APIs at the same time (fewer GeckoTerminal rate-limit errors), arbitrage round-trips (such as a fake small "sell") are filtered better, and if trades had to come from cache the widget says so with the time. The "All pools" totals and per-pool reserves from v3.1 stay.
