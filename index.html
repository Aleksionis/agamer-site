About

Agamer Universe is a Telegram ecosystem built around play and collecting, and it stays on TON. Inside @agameruniversebot you'll find games, a built-in marketplace for Agamer NFTs and Telegram Gifts, and auctions — all connected by the AGAMER coin. Mini games, giveaways and similar continue as before, with coin freezing as planned, plus a hub for games from other developers. The Agamer NFT collection is also available on Getgems.

AGAMER coin

The native coin of Agamer Universe, issued as a jetton on The Open Network (TON). It stays on TON. AGAMER also launches on Solana in Q2 2027.

Admin rights of the jetton contract have been revoked (admin set to the zero address), so no one can mint new tokens or change the contract metadata. Total supply as reported by TonAPI on 13 Oct 2026.

TON AGAMER stays on TON forever as the original token. Trading and pools stay, and LP tokens are burned so liquidity cannot be withdrawn. On Solana, the launch is in Q2 2027, together with the game prototype. The exact listing date is announced once the prototype is ready, together with game trailers. There are no balance snapshots. Before the Solana listing, a swap window is open: holders burn TON AGAMER at 10 to 1 (100 AGAMER on TON gives 10 on Solana), with a minimum of 100 AGAMER per swap to keep bots out. Supply is 999,000 (same as on TON). Of that supply, 10% is reserved for the swap, 20% for the liquidity pool, and 70% is locked by smart contract with public terms. The unswapped part of the swap reserve is frozen with the 70%. There are no hubs on Solana: the coin launches there, and a big Agamer game is announced, which the team builds itself (Q2 2027 at the earliest). It is a 3D open-world game with Solana and Agamer NFTs. The first version is a prototype (playable core: character, controls, a small map, wallet connection, and NFTs shown in the game), then one polished location, then growth into a large open world with multiplayer. The TON-to-Solana swap for Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs opens after the game prototype is released and official Agamer NFT sales on Solana start. A swap window is given then; its length is announced later. After the window closes, swapping a TON Agamer NFT for a Solana one is no longer possible. Only NFTs of rarity Rare, Epic and Legendary can be swapped. VIP, Common and Uncommon NFTs stay on TON forever. Rare to Legendary NFTs were originally designed for the game. The swap is 1:1: the holder gives the TON NFT and receives the same one on Solana. It is one-way and cannot be reversed. Total supply stays 1,000: a part moves to Solana permanently, and a part stays on TON permanently. All Rare, Epic and Legendary NFTs owned by the project and not bought are taken off sale. They will move to Solana and go on sale there only after the prototype is released. Holders of AGAMER on Solana with a minimum balance get access to the closed beta test of the game. The minimum is announced together with the listing date. Everyone chooses what to do with their coin: stay on TON, move to Solana, or have both.
