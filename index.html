About

Agamer Universe is a Telegram ecosystem built around play and collecting. Inside @agameruniversebot you'll find games, a built-in marketplace for Agamer NFTs and Telegram Gifts, and auctions — all connected by the AGAMER coin. The Agamer NFT collection is also available on Getgems.
